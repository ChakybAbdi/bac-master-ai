## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Verified material developments were found.** Because no earlier report exists, they are new to our records, not breaking news: the newest Ministry post is dated 2 November 2026 and is unrelated to the BAC.
- **Most important:** the July 2026 changes to secondary-school subjects, hours and coefficients are **not in force**. Arrêté n° 36 of 9 September 2026 repealed arrêté n° 20 of 28 July 2026, and a Ministry statement of 16 September 2026 says every July arrangement except the primary-school ones is frozen until the Council of Ministers approves it. The 2006 decree on hours and coefficients (arrêté n° 186) remains in force, so BAC 2027 runs on the pre-July framework.
- **Students:** no action is required today. No official registration period, examination date or results date for BAC 2027 has been announced. The dates circulating online are unverified.
- **BAC MASTER AI:** changes are required. Audit the BAC 2027 edition for anything built on the July announcements, load the official 2026-2027 calendar, and prepare (but do not publish) a registration alert.

## 3. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR STUDENTS

**All 3AS students, all streams (BAC 2027)**

- The subject organisation, hours and coefficients are those of the framework in force before July 2026 (arrêté n° 186 of 2006, as amended). The tables of "new coefficients" published in July and August 2026 do not apply.
- No change to revision priorities is justified by the July announcements.
- This run did not re-check the numeric coefficients against the official text, so students should rely on their school for the exact figures.

**Mathématiques and Technique mathématique**

- The addition of computer science in 3AS is not in force. We found no official document making it a BAC 2027 subject.

**Registration (التسجيلات), all candidates**

- No action yet: no official opening date or deadline exists. bac.onec.dz still shows the BAC 2026 results form.
- Last session, the Ministry announced the opening one day in advance (17 November 2025 for 18 November), so the announcement may come at short notice.
- Last session's procedure (not yet confirmed for 2027): school heads registered enrolled candidates; independent candidates (المترشحون الأحرار) registered themselves on bac.onec.dz; fees were paid online only, with the Edahabia card.

**Calendar**

- 3AS test period: 9–13 May 2027. Winter holidays start the evening of 17 December 2026; spring holidays the evening of 18 March 2027.
- The school year started on 21 September 2026, two weeks later than first announced.

**Warning:** BAC 2027 examination dates have not been announced. Do not plan around "7–11 June" or "13–17 June".
